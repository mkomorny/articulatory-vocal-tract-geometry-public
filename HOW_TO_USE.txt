HOW TO USE: articulatory-vocal-tract-geometry
====================================

PURPOSE
  Model estimated vocal tract length (VTL cm), pharyngeal volume, velopharyngeal port
  dynamics, larynx height displacement, and lip protrusion for physical and parametric synthesis.

CAPABILITIES
  - Calculate estimated anatomical VTL (cm) from formant dispersion (Fdisp)
  - Derive VTL warping factor relative to standard reference vocal tracts
  - Measure lip posture: protrusion bias (mm), aperture area (cm2), rounding coefficient
  - Model velopharyngeal port dynamics: coupling area (mm2), nasal pole/zero (FN/ZN)
  - Estimate vertical larynx displacement (mm) during pitch accents and projection
  - Output machine-readable vocal_tract_geometry.json

USE CASES
  - "Calculate the physical vocal tract length and warping factor for this speaker"
  - You are setting up pole-zero nasal resonators in Klatt cascade synthesis
  - You need physical tract parameters for tube-model synthesizers (VocalTractLab)
  - You want to understand why pitch shifts cause nasal twang or brighter resonance

IDEAS
  - Pass VTL warping factor directly to WORLD vocoder in synthesis-control-compiler
  - Pair with formant-diphthong-dynamics to ground formants in physical geometry
  - Use larynx displacement to model high-pitch vocal strain

PROMPTS YOU CAN PASTE
  /vocal-tract-geometry Queens-male-orator

  vocal tract geometry of Steve Jobs

  articulatory profile for this speaker

  model vocal tract dimensions for this voice. Calculate VTL in cm and nasal pole/zeros.

         4. Larynx Posture:
         - Mildly elevated (+3.5mm during emphatic peaks)
         [vocal_tract_geometry.json]

DEFAULT GOALS IF YOU DON'T NAME ONE
  1. Estimated VTL in cm derived from formant dispersion
  2. Lip aperture area and protrusion factor
  3. Velopharyngeal nasal pole/zero acoustic parameters
  4. Machine-readable vocal_tract_geometry.json block
